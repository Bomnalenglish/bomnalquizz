This captured carbon is commonly known as "blue carbon," absorbing from the atmosphere and stored in the ocean. Blue carbon is mainly carbon dioxide that has dissolved directly in the ocean. While "black carbon" is a harmful material produced by burning fossil fuels and a significant contributor to climate change, blue carbon is stored in the ocean, preventing global warming. Nevertheless, the ocean's ability to perform this planet saving function depends on its health. Dr. Duarte and his team propose a practicable action plan to restore ocean health and aid in the planet's decarbonization. Their urgent recommendations include protecting at least 30% of the ocean by 2030, rethinking industrial fishing practices, restoring damaged ecosystems like mangroves and coral reefs, and reducing pollution entered the sea. By prioritizing ocean health, we can unlock its potential as a remedy to climate change and work towards a more sustainable future.
